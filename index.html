2. Data yang Dikumpulkan & Tujuan Penggunaannya

A. Penyimpanan Data Lokal (Offline-First Architecture)

Nǐ Hànzì mengusung prinsip offline-first untuk kenyamanan belajar tanpa harus selalu terhubung ke internet. Data berikut disimpan secara lokal di ruang penyimpanan privat perangkat Anda menggunakan basis data SQLite dan Shared Preferences:

  • Progres Hafalan & Riwayat Belajar: Daftar karakter Hanzi yang sudah dipelajari, status hafalan kartu (Belum Hafal, Menengah, Hafal), tingkat akurasi kuis, dan total skor.
  • Rekor Kuis Time Attack: Waktu terbaik, kombo benar beruntun, dan riwayat skor kuis kecepatan.
  • Preferensi Pengguna: Pilihan tema (Gelap/Terang), kecepatan lafal audio Text-to-Speech (TTS), jadwal pengingat harian, serta pengaturan antarmuka lainnya.

Data lokal ini tersimpan sepenuhnya di perangkat Anda dan tidak dapat diakses oleh aplikasi lain di ponsel Anda.

Aplikasi menyediakan opsi masuk menggunakan Akun Google melalui layanan resmi Google Play Services. Jika Anda memilih masuk, kami hanya menerima informasi profil dasar publik dari Google:

Kami tidak meminta akses ke kontak, pesan, email pribadi, berkas Google Foto, atau data pribadi sensitif lainnya dari akun Google Anda.

2. Information We Collect and How We Use It

A. Local Device Storage (Offline-First Architecture)

Nǐ Hànzì utilizes an offline-first design to provide seamless learning without requiring an active internet connection. The following data is stored locally in your device's private sandbox storage using SQLite database and Shared Preferences:

  • Study Progress & Flashcard Mastery: List of learned Hanzi characters, card mastery status (Learning, Familiar, Mastered), quiz accuracy rates, and total scores.
  • Time Attack Quiz Records: Best completion times, highest correct answer streaks, and speed quiz score history.
  • User Preferences: Theme selection (Dark/Light mode), Text-to-Speech (TTS) speech rate, daily reminder schedule, and user interface preferences.

This local data resides strictly on your physical device and cannot be accessed by other third-party apps on your phone.

B. Google Account (Google Sign-In)

The App provides an optional sign-in mechanism using your Google Account via official Google Play Services. When you choose to sign in, we receive basic public profile information from Google:

  • Email Address: Used as your unique account identifier to synchronize your subscription license status across devices.
  • Display Name & Public Profile Photo: Used solely for visual representation within the App's Settings screen.
  • Google User ID (UID): A unique Firebase Authentication identifier used to securely tie purchased license tiers to your account.

We do not request access to your contacts, private emails, Google Photos, or any sensitive personal files from your Google Account.

C. Google Play Subscriptions & License Management

Nǐ Hànzì offers official subscription tiers through Google Play In-App Billing to unlock advanced content and remove advertisements:

Starter 3-Month Plan (ni_hanzi_sub_3months): Access to HSK 1–2 content and 100% Ad-Free experience (no banners, no interstitials).
Pro 6-Month Semester Plan (ni_hanzi_sub_6months): Full access to HSK 1 through HSK 6 (Mastery Pack), 100% ad-free, and all premium features unlocked.
Pro 1-Year Master Plan (ni_hanzi_sub_1year): Full annual access to HSK 1–6 at the best value (save 25%), completely ad-free.

When you subscribe, we maintain a secure transaction audit entry on Cloud Firestore (Google Play Order ID, Transaction Date, Purchase Token, and Active License Tier) to allow license restoration whenever you reinstall the app or switch devices.

🛡️ Guaranteed Financial Security

We NEVER access, collect, or store your credit card details, debit card numbers, bank account information, or payment credentials. All payment processing is conducted securely, privately, and directly by the Google Play Store.

Managing & Cancelling Subscriptions: All subscriptions automatically renew unless cancelled by the user. You can manage or cancel your active subscription at any time via the Google Play Store app > Profile Icon > Payments & Subscriptions > Subscriptions.

D. Study Progress Backup to Google Drive (Optional)

The App offers an optional feature allowing users to back up their local study database to their personal Google Drive storage:

  • Restricted Scope (appDataFolder): Nǐ Hànzì strictly requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata permission scope, which restricts access exclusively to an isolated, hidden Application Data folder created by Nǐ Hànzì on your Google Drive.
  • Strict Privacy: Nǐ Hànzì CANNOT view, read, search, edit, or delete any of your documents, photos, videos, or personal files stored on your Google Drive.

E. Advertisements & Device Identifiers (Google AdMob)

For users on the free tier, the App serves advertisements through the official Google AdMob SDK to support ongoing development:

  • Banner Ads: Displayed at the bottom of study and quiz screens in an unobtrusive manner.
  • Interstitial Ads (Full-Screen Transitions): Displayed at natural stopping points, such as upon completing a Time Attack quiz session before the final score summary is presented.
  • Rewarded Ads (Rewarded Videos): Displayed strictly when users deliberately opt-in to watch a short sponsored video to unlock bonus learning perks (such as extra cards in Fullscreen mode beyond the daily free quota).
✨ 100% Ad-Free for Subscribers

For users with an active paid subscription (Starter or Pro), all advertising units (Banners, Interstitials, and Rewarded Ad requirements) are completely and automatically disabled.

Google AdMob may collect anonymous device identifiers including the Android Advertising ID (GAID), truncated IP addresses, and ad interaction telemetry in compliance with Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset or opt out of ad personalization at any time under your device's Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.

F. Chinese Pronunciation Audio (Text-to-Speech)

Hanzi character and sentence pronunciation playback uses your device's built-in Android Text-to-Speech (TTS) engine (such as Google Speech Services). Audio synthesis is processed locally on your device without recording your voice or transmitting audio recordings over the internet.
