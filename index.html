2. Data yang Dikumpulkan & Tujuan Penggunaannya

A. Akun Google (Google Sign-In)

Saat Anda masuk menggunakan akun Google, kami menerima informasi profil publik dasar dari Google:

  • Alamat Email: Digunakan sebagai identifikasi akun unik Anda.
  • Nama Tampilan & Foto Profil Publik: Hanya digunakan untuk ditampilkan di menu profil Pengaturan aplikasi.

B. Data Pembelian & Lisensi (In-App Purchases)

Jika Anda membeli lisensi Master Pro atau Starter melalui Google Play Billing, kami mencatat ringkasan transaksi (Order ID, Token Pembelian, Tanggal, dan Status Lisensi) pada Cloud Firestore kami.

Kami TIDAK PERNAH mengakses, mengumpulkan, atau menyimpan nomor kartu kredit, detail rekening, atau informasi pembayaran Anda. Seluruh transaksi finansial ditangani secara langsung dan aman oleh Google Play Store.

C. Pencadangan Progres Belajar ke Google Drive (Opsional)

Aplikasi menyediakan fitur opsional untuk mencadangkan data riwayat belajar Anda ke akun Google Drive pribadi Anda.

  • Ruang Lingkup Khusus (appDataFolder): Aplikasi hanya meminta izin untuk menyimpan berkas cadangan ke dalam folder privat aplikasi di Google Drive Anda.
  • Privasi Penuh: Nǐ Hànzì TIDAK DAPAT melihat, membaca, atau mengakses foto, dokumen, atau berkas pribadi lainnya yang tersimpan di Google Drive Anda.

D. Iklan & Pengidentifikasi Perangkat (Google AdMob)

Untuk pengguna versi gratis, aplikasi menayangkan iklan menggunakan Google AdMob SDK. Google AdMob dapat mengumpulkan pengidentifikasi perangkat anonim (seperti Android Advertising ID) untuk statistik penayangan iklan sesuai dengan Kebijakan Privasi Google.

5. Hak Pengguna & Penghapusan Data (Data Deletion)

Google Play Store mewajibkan pengembang memberikan akses penghapusan data secara transparan:

1. Introduction

Welcome to Nǐ Hànzì ("the App"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how your data is collected, used, stored, and protected when using the Nǐ Hànzì Chinese language learning application.

By downloading, installing, or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect and How We Use It

A. Google Account (Google Sign-In)

When you choose to sign in with your Google account, we receive basic public profile information from Google:

  • Email Address: Used as your unique account identifier.
  • Display Name & Public Profile Photo: Used solely to display your profile in the App's Settings menu.
  • User ID (UID): Used to securely attach and verify your purchased license tier.

B. In-App Purchases & License Audit

If you purchase a Master Pro or Starter license through Google Play In-App Billing, we record a transaction audit entry (Order ID, Purchase Token, Date, and License Tier) in our Cloud Firestore database.

🛡️ Financial Security Guaranteed

We NEVER access, collect, or store your credit card numbers, bank account details, or payment credentials. All financial payment processing is handled directly and securely by the Google Play Store.

C. Study Progress Backup to Google Drive (Optional)

The App provides an optional feature allowing you to backup and restore your study progress data using your personal Google Drive account.

  • Restricted Scope (appDataFolder): The App only requests permission to read and write in its own private, isolated Application Data folder on your Google Drive.
  • Strict Privacy: Nǐ Hànzì CANNOT view, read, modify, or delete any of your photos, documents, or personal files stored on Google Drive.

D. Advertisements & Device Identifiers (Google AdMob)

For users on the free tier, the App may display ads using the Google AdMob SDK. Google AdMob may collect anonymous device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID) and crash logs to serve non-intrusive ads in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Android Permissions Used

Permission Purpose
INTERNET Required for Firebase Authentication, Google Drive backup, and AdMob ads.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Checks whether internet connectivity is available before attempting cloud sync.
BILLING Enables in-app license purchases via official Google Play Billing.
WAKE_LOCK Ensures smooth playback of Chinese Text-to-Speech (TTS) pronunciation audio.

4. Third-Party Data Sharing

We NEVER SELL or rent your personal data to any third parties. Your data is only processed by the following trusted Google infrastructure services:

  • Google Play Services: Core operating system authentication and billing services.
  • Firebase Authentication & Cloud Firestore: User account identity and license verification.
  • Google Drive API: Direct transmission of user study progress backups.
  • Google AdMob: Advertising services for the free tier.

5. User Rights & Data Deletion Policy

In full compliance with Google Play Store Developer Policies, users have complete control over their data:

  • Local Data Deletion: You can clear your study flashcard progress at any time via Settings > Sign Out > "Clear & Sign Out" or the Reset Progress button.
  • Drive Backup Deletion: You can delete your backup file within the app or from your Google Drive web console under Settings > Manage Apps.
  • Cloud Account Deletion Request: You have the right to request permanent deletion of your Firebase account and purchase records by sending an email to nakitostudio01@gmail.com with the subject "Nǐ Hànzì Account Deletion Request". Requests are fulfilled within 7 business days.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

The App is designed for general educational purposes. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. If you believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately for deletion.
